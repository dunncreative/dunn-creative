Kinetic was investing real budget into paid social but feeding it just a few
static ads. The creative fatigued quickly, the algorithm had nothing fresh to
optimize against, and customer acquisition costs crept up week over week. The
media team needed volume without losing the brand.

We built a creative system, not a one-off batch: a flexible template framework
plus an AI-accelerated production pipeline that turns each concept into dozens of
on-brand statics and motion variants — sized for every placement, ready to test.
Winners get scaled, losers get cut, and the pipeline keeps the queue full.

With a steady stream of fresh, on-brand creative to test, the media team could
finally optimize on creative as a lever. Fatigue stopped capping performance, and
acquisition costs moved in the right direction.
