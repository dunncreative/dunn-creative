Lumen had a strong product but a website working against it. Pages loaded slowly,
the messaging buried the actual value under clinical jargon, and the marketing
team couldn't make changes without a developer. Demo requests had gone flat even
as ad spend held steady.

We rebuilt the site from the messaging up: a sharpened value proposition, a clear
path from landing to demo request, and a design system that feels credible to
clinicians and approachable to operators. Under the hood, a static-first build
and a friendly CMS mean sub-second loads and edits the team can ship themselves.

The new site reframed Lumen's story and removed the friction around its primary
conversion. Demo requests climbed, and the marketing team finally owns the page
instead of waiting on a queue.
