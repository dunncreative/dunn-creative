Veridian was raising at a strong moment, but their deck didn't show it. Forty
slides of dense specs and screenshots buried the thesis, and investors were
forming opinions before the team ever reached the ask. Every pitch started from a
deficit.

We restructured the story first — one defensible thesis, a clear arc from problem
to proof to ask — then designed slides that carried it: disciplined typography,
purposeful data visualization, and room for the numbers to breathe. We delivered
an editable master template so the team could keep building on-brand slides
without us.

The narrative did the heavy lifting. Veridian closed their Series B, and the same
system now powers their sales and recruiting decks — one story, told
consistently, everywhere it counts.
