Northwind built a devoted local following on the quality of the roast, but the
brand never matched the product in the cup. The wordmark was thin and forgettable,
the palette leaned cold, and on a crowded retail shelf the bags simply
disappeared next to bigger names.

We rebuilt the identity around the idea of earned warmth — a confident custom
wordmark, a palette of deep roast browns and a single bright accent, and a
flexible label system that scales across single-origin and blend lines. The mark
works as a stamp, a pin, and a 40-foot wall just as well as it does on a 12oz bag.

The rebrand gave the team a system they could run themselves, and it changed how
buyers saw them. Within a season, wholesale conversations got easier and the
brand finally looked like the roast tasted.
