Meridian Capital Partners had a strong track record but a brand that lagged
behind it. Their materials read like a regional advisory shop, while their
ambitions — and their LP targets — were squarely institutional. The gap was
costing them credibility in the room.

We rebuilt the identity from the positioning up: a restrained, confident system
in deep navy and accent teal, paired with a typographic voice that signaled
discipline rather than flash. Every touchpoint — from the wordmark to the data
visualization style — was designed to feel like it belonged in front of a
pension fund's investment committee.

The centerpiece was the fund's investor narrative. We restructured the pitch
around a single, defensible thesis and designed a deck that carried it cleanly.
The new system gave the partners something they hadn't had before: materials
that matched the quality of the team behind them. They closed a $40M fund — their
largest to date — within two quarters of the relaunch.
